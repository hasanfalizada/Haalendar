# time.event_days.v1 — examples

1. **Birthday** — an all-day event on 9 May stays on 9 May for viewers in every zone (all-day events are date-only).
2. **Late call across zones** — 23:00–23:30 in Berlin appears on the next day for a colleague in Baku (+04:00) who shares the calendar.
3. **Evening ending at midnight** — 22:00–00:00 occupies only the evening's day, not the following one.
4. **Night shift** — 22:00–06:00 in Tokyo covers two days.
