# Settings

+++===+++ 2026-11-26 — Hasan Alizada — HAAL-153 (D-129, stage 2). **Settings** in the bar on the left or in the account
menu; they follow you to every device.

## Preferences

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Theme | System, Light or Dark |
| Time format | 24-hour or 12-hour |
| Week starts on | The first day of weeks and of the small month |
| Default color for new items | The colour new events, notes and cards get |
| Agenda shows (days) | How many days the agenda view lists, 1 to 365 |
| Events of a day | The order of a day's events: by time or by colour |
| Week A/B | A label that alternates every week (for example for shifts or school weeks), counted from a week you choose |

## Profile

Your name, your picture (PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 2 MB; it is cut to a square and its location data removed), the
language (English for now) and your **time zone**, in which all times are shown.

## Security

- **Change password:** your other devices are signed out.
- **Two-factor authentication:** **Set up**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app, type the 6-digit code it shows.
  Then keep the **recovery codes** that appear once: each one signs you in once if you lose your phone. **New
  recovery codes** replaces them; **Turn off** ends two-factor sign-in.
- **Signed-in devices:** where you are signed in and when each was last active; sign out one of them, or **Sign out
  all other devices** — for example after losing a phone.

Changing these asks for your password again when you last entered it more than 15 minutes ago.

## Sharing

What you shared and what others shared with you, with their permission; change or remove access, or leave
([Across the app](across-the-app.md#sharing)).

## Your data

A copy of everything you own, as a ZIP archive: all records in one JSON file (deleted ones included), your calendars
as `.ics` files and, if you want, your images. Choose the modules, **Create export**, confirm your password; the
archive is prepared in the background, the bell (and an e-mail, if the installation sends mail) tells you when it is
ready, and it can be downloaded for 7 days. The format: [data export](../../export/README.md).

## Notifications

**Browser notifications** show reminders even while Haalendar is closed. They are set for each device and browser:
**Turn on** here, and allow them when the browser asks. If the browser blocks them, allow notifications for Haalendar
in its site settings first. In the app the bell shows them in any case.

## Sync

The state of sync on this device, when it last synced, **Sync now**, and the conflicts waiting for your decision
([Sync and conflicts](sync.md)).
